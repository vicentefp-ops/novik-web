Clear answers. Better decisions.

Everything you need to know about Novik AI.

01What is Novik?+

Novik is an AI-powered clinical decision support tool built for dentistry. It synthesises patient data with medical literature to support safe treatment planning.

02Who can use Novik?+

Licensed dentists and dental care professionals, plus dental students in supervised settings.

03Does Novik diagnose?+

No. Novik provides evidence-based suggestions and dose adjustments. Diagnosis and final decisions always remain with the treating clinician.

04Is my data secure?+

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted to authorised users, and we follow EU GDPR requirements. Review the privacy policy for the full terms. Do not enter patient identifiers.

05Is Novik HIPAA compliant?+

Novik is exploring HIPAA alignment for use in the United States. Its terms specify that the platform is designed for de-identified data and does not collect or maintain protected health information. Do not enter patient identifiers.

06Is Novik free?+

Yes. During open beta the core features are free. Future paid tiers may add higher limits and premium capabilities.
